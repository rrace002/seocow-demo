Approach

Individual securities, customized mandates, and a team investment process.

AHB's differentiated positioning is active management of customized SMAs, with portfolios invested in individual stocks and bonds. Client guidelines, tax considerations, account restrictions, and transition needs can be reflected directly in the account.

  • Investment Policy Committee team approach.
  • Tax-efficient transition planning for existing holdings.
  • Separate account transparency and individual security ownership.
  • Nationwide availability through advisor and wrap platform relationships.

Who AHB serves

AHB works with advisors and end clients who need institutional discipline with account-level customization.

  • Financial advisors and platform partners.
  • High-net-worth individuals and families.
  • Nonprofits, foundations, corporations, and fiduciary committees.
