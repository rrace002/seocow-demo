Questions contractors actually ask

Straight answers

Why would I need 80 or 120 pages?

You don't read them — Google does. Each page is built to answer one specific search ("faucet repair in [town]"). More pages means you're entered in more races. Your workload is identical at every tier: one questionnaire.

Do I have to write anything?

No. You answer questions about your business once (about 45 minutes). We produce every page, professionally written and structured. You review and approve before anything goes live.

Who owns the website?

You do — domain, content, design, everything, whether you paid one-time or monthly. It's written into the agreement. This is the question the big marketing firms hope you never ask them.

How is it this fast and this affordable?

Your site is built on the Near Me OS engine: the structure, technical SEO, and page architecture are engineered once, at production quality, instead of rebuilt by hand for every client the way agencies do. Your details make it yours; the engine keeps the cost down. The savings go to you.

Will it guarantee me leads?

No honest company guarantees rankings — walk away from anyone who does. What we guarantee is the infrastructure: pages built the way Google rewards, a quote form on every one, and tracking so you can see exactly which pages produce calls. You can't win searches you don't have pages for.

What if I already have a website?

We can build on your existing domain so you keep any history it has — or launch fresh alongside it. Either way, count your current pages first. If it's under 10, you now know why the phone is quiet.
