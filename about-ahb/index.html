About AHB

Abner Herrman & Brock is a Jersey City investment management firm focused on customized SMAs.

Abner Herrman & Brock LLC was founded in 1981 and is headquartered at Harborside in Jersey City, NJ. The firm serves advisors, high-net-worth investors, families, nonprofits, and corporations through actively managed separate account strategies.
